## 📖 About

PetPals is an open-source Personal AI Executive Assistant designed to support people with executive functioning challenges.

Unlike a traditional to-do list, PetPals analyses the user's context, available time, energy level and priorities to recommend what should be done next.

The project follows a modular architecture and is being developed as a long-term learning project covering:

- Python
- Software Architecture
- SQLAlchemy
- Artificial Intelligence
- Local LLMs
- Agentic Systems
- Executive Function Support

---

## ✨ Vision

PetPals is not intended to be just another chatbot.

The goal is to become an intelligent executive assistant capable of helping users:

- 🧠 Remember important information
- 📅 Organise daily routines
- ✅ Manage projects and tasks
- ⚡ Adapt suggestions according to energy level
- 🎯 Reduce cognitive overload
- 🤖 Work completely offline using local AI models

## 💡 Inspiration

Many productivity applications assume that users always have the same amount of energy, attention and motivation.

PetPals takes a different approach.

Instead of asking only:

> "What do you have to do?"

it also asks:

- How much energy do you have?
- How much time do you have?
- What environment are you currently in?
- What is your cognitive load today?

The objective is to reduce executive overload by adapting recommendations to the user's current context.

---
## ❤️ Why PetPals?

PetPals was born from a simple question:

> "What if software could adapt to the way people think, instead of expecting people to adapt to software?"

As someone interested in accessibility, education and neurodiversity, I wanted to build an assistant that supports executive functioning rather than simply managing tasks.

The goal is not only to build software, but to explore how technology can become a practical and respectful cognitive support tool for everyday life.
